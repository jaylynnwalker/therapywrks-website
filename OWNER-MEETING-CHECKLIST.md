# TherapyWrks Website — Owner Meeting Checklist

Use this checklist to confirm the content and functionality needed before the website is finalized.

## Appointments and contact

- [ ] What is the preferred appointment path: website inquiry form, phone call, scheduling platform, or a combination?
- [ ] What should the main button say: “Request an Appointment,” “Schedule a Consultation,” or something else?
- [ ] Where should appointment requests be delivered?
- [ ] What phone number and email address should appear on the website?
- [ ] What office hours or typical response time should be listed?
- [ ] Should the website display a physical address, general service area, or neither?

## Client portal

- [ ] Is a Client Portal link needed?
- [ ] If so, what is the correct portal URL?
- [ ] Should the portal link be prominent or placed quietly in the footer/navigation?

## Practice details

- [ ] Confirm Thomala’s full professional name, credentials, license type, and license number if it should be displayed.
- [ ] Confirm how many years of experience should be stated.
- [ ] Confirm the states where Thomala is licensed and where virtual sessions may be provided.
- [ ] Confirm which ages and client groups are currently served: children, adolescents, adults, couples, families, or groups.
- [ ] Confirm whether the existing About and counselor biography wording is approved.

## Services and payment

- [ ] Confirm each current service, including individual therapy, couples counseling, group therapy, and virtual sessions.
- [ ] Confirm all areas of focus listed on the website.
- [ ] Verify each insurance provider: Blue Cross Blue Shield, Aetna, Cigna, UnitedHealthcare, and Optum.
- [ ] Confirm whether self-pay is offered and whether rates should be published.
- [ ] Ask whether a free consultation is offered.
- [ ] Confirm the cancellation policy and required notice period.

## Blog

- [ ] Who will write and approve blog posts?
- [ ] How often should new posts be published?
- [ ] What topics or categories should the blog cover?
- [ ] Does the owner want to update posts herself through a content-management system, or will a developer add them?
- [ ] Are there two or three initial post ideas available for launch?
- [ ] Should posts include an author bio, publish date, disclaimer, or related resources?

## Photos and brand

- [ ] Collect the approved high-resolution headshot and any office, lifestyle, or brand photos.
- [ ] Confirm permission to use every supplied photo on the public website.
- [ ] Ask whether the current TherapyWrks logo is the final version and whether a transparent-background version exists.
- [ ] Confirm that the current taupe, deep green, black, and white color palette is approved.
- [ ] Ask whether any websites reflect the owner’s preferred visual style.

## Policies and trust

- [ ] Does the practice have an approved privacy policy and website disclaimer?
- [ ] Confirm whether the inquiry form is appropriate for the practice’s privacy requirements.
- [ ] Confirm what visitors should and should not share in the inquiry form.
- [ ] Verify the crisis-resource wording and phone/text information.
- [ ] Ask whether an accessibility statement is desired.
- [ ] Confirm whether testimonials may be used and whether written permission exists for them.

## Final review

- [ ] Confirm the legal business name and copyright wording.
- [ ] Confirm the website domain and hosting account access.
- [ ] Decide who receives form-submission and website-error notifications.
- [ ] Confirm who has final approval before the refreshed site is published.
